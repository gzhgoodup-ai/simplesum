Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 3, 2026

SimpleSum (“the Extension”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data We Do NOT Collect

SimpleSum does not collect, store, or transmit any of the following:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Browsing history or visited URLs
  • Page content you choose to summarize (it is sent directly to your chosen AI provider, never to us)
  • API keys (stored locally in Chrome’s built-in storage)
  • Usage analytics, telemetry, or crash reports

2. Data Stored Locally

The Extension stores the following data exclusively on your device via Chrome’s chrome.storage API:

  • Your configured API provider settings (API key, base URL, model ID)
  • Daily summary usage count (to enforce the free tier limit)
  • Your license key (if you have purchased SimpleSum Pro)

None of this data is synced to any external server owned by SimpleSum. Chrome may sync chrome.storage.sync data across your own devices via your Google account; this is controlled by Chrome, not by us.

3. Data Sent to Third Parties

When you summarize a webpage, the extracted page content is sent directly from your browser to the AI provider you have configured (DeepSeek, Anthropic, OpenAI, or a custom endpoint). This connection is between you and the provider. SimpleSum does not intercept, log, or proxy these requests.

Please refer to each provider’s privacy policy:

  • DeepSeek Privacy Policy
  • Anthropic Privacy Policy
  • OpenAI Privacy Policy

4. Permissions

The Extension requests the following Chrome permissions:

  • activeTab — to access the current page’s content only when you trigger a summary
  • storage — to save your settings and daily usage count locally
  • clipboardWrite — to copy the summary to your clipboard when you click “Copy”
  • Host permissions (https://*/*) — to send API requests to your chosen AI provider

5. License & Payment

SimpleSum Pro subscriptions are processed through Lemon Squeezy. SimpleSum does not handle or store your payment information. Your license key is validated locally using a checksum algorithm; no external validation server is used.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at privacy@simplesum.ai.
